ABOUT

The Tata Group is a global Indian conglomerate founded in 1868 by Jamsetji Tata. It is a multinational company with operations in over 100 countries across six continents, focusing on industries like steel, automobiles, IT, consumer products, and hospitality. Headquartered in Mumbai, the group has a mission to improve the quality of life for communities and operates with a focus on ethical practices, with Tata Sons as its principal holding company.
